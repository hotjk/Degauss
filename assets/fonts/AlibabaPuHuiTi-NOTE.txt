AlibabaPuHuiTi-3-55-Regular.ttf is embedded for personal Simplified Chinese
game-name rendering in this Degauss fork.

It is registered as Appearance → Text → PuHui (settings/theme: font = "puhui")
and baked at 12px (Slint embed seed) + 26px (list titles) in
ui/font-packs/puhui.slint. Smooth / Pixel / Smooth 2 / Pixel 2 do not import
this face and do not bake Han glyphs.

Do not redistribute the font file. Official Alibaba PuHuiTi terms apply.
